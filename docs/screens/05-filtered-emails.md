## Toolbar

## Email Row
☆ | Sender (bold if unread) | [BADGE] | Subject | Preview… | Timestamp

## Categories & Badges

- LEAD GEN — blue pill
- FINANCE — grey pill
- UPDATE — neutral pill

## Behavior
- Click star → toggles ☆/★
- Click row → marks as read (future: opens detail)
- Unread: white bg, bold sender; Read: slightly dimmed
